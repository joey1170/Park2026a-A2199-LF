# Data for Surface Brightness Fit
s_id         STRING: Object ID (DR7) from Simard+12 (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/#abs/2011ApJS..196...11S/abstract)
s_sersic      FLOAT: Galaxy Sersic index (from GIM2D fit)
s_sersic_err  FLOAT: Uncertainty in ng
s_mreff       FLOAT: r-band galaxy semi-major axis, half-light radius (arcsec) (from GIM2D fit)
s_reff        FLOAT: r-band galaxy circular half-light radius (arcsec) (from GIM2D fit)
l_id         STRING: ID from https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/#abs/2012MNRAS.421.2277L/abstract
l_sersic      FLOAT: Galaxy Sersic index 
l_sersic_err  FLOAT: Uncertainty in ng
